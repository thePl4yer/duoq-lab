# Hero artwork

`duo-hero.webp` is original artwork created with the built-in Imagegen tool for this website. The user supplied a personal anime Rocket League illustration as a style reference. Their reference file and local filesystem path are not distributed with the website.

Final prompt: A panoramic original anime dashboard hero with two clearly adult esports teammates in black racing hoodies: a black-haired teammate with gold trim and a grey-purple-haired teammate with a sleeping white cat on her head. Playful shoulder bump, a stolen golden boost orb and a consolation chocolate chip cookie, a small rocket car and football. Charcoal, midnight cobalt and warm gold; dynamic ink linework, small manga reaction, negative space on the left. No pink, slogans, logos, player controls or watermark. Friendly, fully clothed, nonsexual rivalry.

The source PNG was converted to WebP for delivery, without changing the composition.

`duo-hero-phone.webp` is the same artwork scaled to 1080x360 for phone screens.
